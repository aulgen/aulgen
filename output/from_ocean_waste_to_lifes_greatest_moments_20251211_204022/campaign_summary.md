
# Campaign Summary: From Ocean Waste to Life's Greatest Moments

## Concept
Transform ocean pollution into personal hydration transformation, showing how choosing EcoBreeze creates a ripple effect from ocean cleanup to optimal health and peak life performance.

## Story Arc
We follow the journey from polluted ocean plastic being transformed into a beautiful EcoBreeze bottle, then witness how this smart companion empowers a young professional to stay perfectly hydrated through life's most demanding and rewarding moments, creating a positive impact cycle.

## Scene Overview
5 scenes, 30 seconds total

## Target Platforms
Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok

## Assets Generated
- 5 Image Generation Prompts
- 5 Scene Scripts
- 1 Full Cohesive Script
