Color palette: deep ocean blues (navy, teal) transitioning to bright whites and subtle golds. Mood: ethereal, hopeful, transformative. Style: high-end commercial photography with CGI elements, pristine clarity, dramatic lighting contrast between dark depths and bright surface. The EcoBreeze bottle should appear pristine and modern as it emerges, creating a stark visual metaphor for transformation. Emphasize fluid motion throughout - nothing should feel static or abrupt. The transformation should feel organic and hopeful, not artificial.
